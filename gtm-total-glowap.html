What gets audited

Every layer of your GTM stack

Four areas, three questions each. Every answer is backed by evidence from your own systems.

CRM & Revenue Data

  • Is your CRM data accurate and complete?
  • Do stages reflect how deals actually move?
  • Does leadership trust the pipeline numbers?

Outbound & Prospecting

  • Are your emails landing in the inbox?
  • Is your ICP definition operationalized in your tools?
  • What is your actual reply rate and where are you losing prospects?

Analytics & Attribution

  • Does your GA4 track what matters?
  • Can you connect a closed deal back to its original marketing source?
  • Are your dashboards automated or manually assembled?

Tool Stack & Costs

  • What tools are you paying for but not using?
  • Where are there overlaps in your stack?
  • What is the gap between your current tools and the tier you need to be at?
