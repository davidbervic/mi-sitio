What gets audited

Every layer of your GTM stack

Four areas, three questions each. Every answer is backed by evidence from your own systems.

CRM & Revenue Data

  • Is your pipeline data accurate enough to forecast from?
  • Are deals progressing through stages that reflect real buying behavior?
  • Is your CRM the system of record or a reporting afterthought?

Outbound & Prospecting

  • Are your emails landing in the inbox?
  • Is your ICP defined well enough to build a list from?
  • Is your outbound motion systematic or ad hoc?

Analytics & Attribution

  • Do you know which channels generate your best customers?
  • Does your GA4 data match your CRM?
  • Can you answer attribution questions without pulling reports manually?

Tool Stack & Costs

  • Are you paying for tools nobody uses?
  • Are there gaps your team is working around manually?
  • Is your stack integrated or are people copy-pasting between tools?
