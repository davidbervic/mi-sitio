What this program does, and doesn't, do

This program automates manual GTM tasks. It saves your team 5 to 10 hours per week. It does not directly generate leads or close deals. If your goal is more pipeline, start with Modern Outbound or Revenue Architecture. If your goal is getting more output from the team you already have, this is for you.

Who it's for

This program is for you if...

Your team spends more than 5 hours a week on tasks that could be automated
CRM notes are inconsistent because nobody wants to type them after every call
High-value leads get missed because monitoring is done manually
Pre-call research is done from scratch, every single time
You're producing content manually when AI could handle the first draft
