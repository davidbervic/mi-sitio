Foundation layer
the base layer

It starts with a CRM you can trust.

Nothing else works without this. The CRM is where every contact, deal, and interaction lives, the single source of truth that captures the data and defines the customer journey. Get it right, and every layer above it has something solid to stand on.

The ground everything is built on

Sourcing, scoring, enrichment, signals, automation, reporting: none of it means anything on a messy CRM. I build the foundation first, a clean, well-structured data model that captures every interaction and maps the full path from first touch to closed deal, across whichever platform the business runs on.

Source layer
chapter 1 of 6

Every lead source, feeding one clean pipeline.

More pipeline usually means more people chasing it. Here it doesn't: the volume comes from the channels, the qualifying comes from the system.

Many tools, one language in the CRM

Each tool sends data its own way: different fields, different formats, its own logic. Left unmanaged, they turn the CRM into a mess of mismatched records. The sourcing system maps every one into a single structure, so a lead from Apollo and a lead from Meta land as the same clean, comparable record, attributed to its true source and ready to work.

Two scores, not a gut feeling

Every lead gets two scores from models built on real conversion data: one for how well it fits the ICP, one for how engaged it is. Together they place each lead on a map, so the team knows exactly what to do with it, and what to skip.

◈ Fit scoreHow closely the lead matches the ideal customer.
◆ Engage scoreHow actively the lead is interacting, with time decay.

And because priority is set here, the next layer only enriches the leads worth enriching, no wasted credits on contacts that won't convert.

Enrich the record, prepare the rep.

A rep who knows the company, the role, and the buying signal closes better than one working from a name and an email. Enrichment gives them that context, running only on the leads the score already flagged as worth it.

Signal layer
chapter 4 of 6

Buying signals, the perfect timing for your reps.

Timing wins deals. The system watches for intent, an email click, a pricing-page visit, a buying signal, and pings the rep the moment it happens, not three days later when it's cold.

Intent, the moment it happens

Internal HubSpot activity like email clicks and web visits, combined with intent data from Amplemarket and Clay, gets read for behavior that actually matters to the business. When a lead moves, the rep gets a notification, and reaches out while the interest is still warm.
