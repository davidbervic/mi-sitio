How it works

Five phases. Fixed scope. You own everything at the end.

Every engagement follows the same structure. What adapts to your business is the content inside each phase.

01
Map
We audit your CRM, document what exists, and identify every gap. You get a written summary of what needs to be built and why before any hands-on work begins.
02
Design
We translate the audit into a build plan. Stage definitions, scoring logic, dashboard views, and property structure are confirmed with you before we touch anything.
03
Deploy
The build happens inside your actual CRM, not a staging environment. Every deliverable is configured in your system. You can watch it take shape in real time.
04
Activate
A live walkthrough with your team. We cover what was built, why it works the way it does, and how to maintain it. Not a training video. A working session.Included in every tier
05
Measure
A 30-day check-in on data quality and adoption. If something isn't working the way it should, we fix it. This is built into every engagement, not an upsell.Included in every tier
